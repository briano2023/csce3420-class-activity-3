Why I'm a Mavericks Fan

I've followed the Dallas Mavericks for years, and being a fan has meant riding out some major changes to the roster, including big trades that reshaped the entire team.

The 2025-26 season introduced Cooper Flagg, the No. 1 overall pick, who went on to win Rookie of the Year while adjusting to a roster that was still finding its identity after a difficult stretch.

Even during a rebuilding season, watching young talent develop alongside veteran presence is what keeps me invested in this team every year.
