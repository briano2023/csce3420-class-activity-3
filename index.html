The Beginnings

1974

Ernő Rubik, an architect professor, creates the first ever Rubik cube model using rubber bands and woods to demonstrate to his students three-dimensional geometry. It took him a little over a month to solve it fully and the first name given to the cube by Rubik was the "Magic Cube".

The Buildup

1977

The Magic cube is sold in toy shops but its startup isn't too popular. The main buyers consisted of mathematicians and curious buyers wanting to know how such a contraption could be solved.

Basic Steps to Solve a 3x3 Rubik's Cube

  1. Solve the white cross
  2. Solve the white corners
  3. Solve the middle layer edges
  4. Solve the yellow cross
  5. Position and orient the last layer

Fun fact: The fastest time a Rubik's cube has ever been solved is under 4 seconds!
